nix-purifiers

A purifier brings locked, Nix-resolved dependencies to your build tool. Install its plugin or activate its integration, lock the project, and keep using your normal build, test and editor commands. Your development workflow runs outside the Nix sandbox. Building the whole project in the sandbox is an optional next step, using a Nix definition in the project or in an external packaging repository.
